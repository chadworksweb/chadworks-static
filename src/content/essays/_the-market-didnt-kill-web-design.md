Builds on Payton Clark Smith's polarization thesis.

**The argument:** The market split into commodity builders (race to the bottom) and experience designers (race to the top). The middle — the $5K "pretty good" website — is dead. CF chose the top.

**Why we scrapped pay-monthly websites:** Not because they're bad business. Because they're average business. And average doesn't survive the split.

**The proof:** Portfolio showing the range — RSLgo (functional, fast, affordable) to Oak Court Press (immersive, atmospheric, experiential). Even the "simple" builds are custom-coded. The floor is higher.
