# chadworks

> Websites and visibility, built with luxury-grade craft. Chad Lewine custom-builds fast, search-ready websites and helps clients choose how the site gets built (custom-coded, WordPress, ecommerce, or Shopify), then keeps them visible in classic search and AI assistants. Client results include a Pennsylvania criminal-defense firm appearing in Google's AI Overview and a Brooklyn psychologist named in AI answers.
